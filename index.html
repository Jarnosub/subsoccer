RULES & GUIDE

OFFICIAL RULES

1
START
The winner of Rock/Paper/Scissors starts.
2
KICK-OFF
Kick-off must be taken inside your own goal area.
3
POSITIONING
Both buttocks must remain on the bench. Hands must not enter the play area and should be kept on the table or on the sides of the benches.
4
BALL OUT OF PLAY
If the ball leaves the table, play resumes with a kick-off by the player from whose side the ball went out.
5
WINNER
The first player to score 3 goals wins.

HOW TO HOST A TOURNAMENT

1
SELECT 2-8 PLAYERS
Enter player names and start the tournament.
2
AUTOMATIC BRACKETS
Subsoccer GO shuffles matches and generates the tournament bracket automatically. If the player count is uneven (e.g. 3 or 5 players), the app assigns free passes (BYE) for the first round.
3
MATCH WINNER & FORFEIT
Mark the winner by clicking the player's name in the bracket. If a player forfeits, mark the opponent as the winner.
4
LARGE TOURNAMENTS
Host multiple separate qualifying pools (max 8 players per pool). Choose the top 1–4 players from each pool and create a new "finals tournament" in the app to crown the overall champion.
