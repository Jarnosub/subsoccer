## 2. Product Ecosystem Breakdown

1. **📱 Mobile Tournament (Core Product):**
   * Digital tournament organizer and matchmaking app for physical Subsoccer tables and mobile players.
   * 90%+ of total platform volume.
   * Enables local 1v1 and multiplayer knockout tournaments with live score tracking.

2. **🎮 Subsoccer GO (Kiosk & Web Arcade):**
   * Fast arcade game for public kiosks, smart tables, and web browser play.
   * First-to-3-goals competitive mode with daily leaderboards and sub-second precision timing.

3. **🕹️ Mini-Game (Promotional QR Play):**
   * Lightweight web minigame embedded in promotional QR codes and marketing channels.

* **🇫🇮 Finland (#3 Global - 1,379 matches):** Subsoccer home market with 294 tournaments and 204 active devices in Helsinki, Riihimäki, and Tampere.
* **🇩🇪 Germany (#6 Global - 406 matches):** Strong growth in Berlin, Munich metro (Höhenkirchen), and Düsseldorf.
* **🇳🇴 Norway (#9 Global - 234 matches):** High tournament activity in Oslo.
* **🇩🇰 Denmark & 🇸🇪 Sweden:** Combined 200+ matches across Copenhagen and Stockholm.

### B. North Africa (Emerging Powerhouse Hub)
* **🇩🇿 Algeria (#2 Global - 1,727 matches):** 2,098 tournaments and intensive community gameplay across Bouira province (Kadiria, Lakhdaria), Djelfa, Medea, and Algiers.
* Highly competitive fast-paced 1v1 play with 88-second median match duration and a 35% comeback rate.

### C. North America (Scale Expansion)
* **🇺🇸 United States (#4 Global - 1,204 matches):** 556 unique devices across major metros (New York, Miami, Los Angeles, Chicago).
* **🇨🇦 Canada (#7 Global - 306 matches):** High tournament participation in Montreal and Vancouver.

### D. Asia-Pacific (High Engagement & Density)
* **🇸🇬 Singapore (#5 Global - 447 matches):** 876 app opens across 149 devices with strong single-city density.
* **🇵🇭 Philippines (#8 Global - 251 matches):** Rapid tournament adoption across Metro Manila and Quezon City.
* **🇮🇩 Indonesia (#10 Global - 157 matches):** Steady growth in Jakarta metro.
* **🇯🇵 Japan, 🇭🇰 Hong Kong, 🇨🇳 China, 🇹🇼 Taiwan:** High engagement urban competitive hubs.

## 3. Key Observations for Investors & Analysts
1. **Global Footprint:** Top 5 countries span Europe (UK, Finland), Africa (Algeria), North America (USA), and Asia (Singapore), proving universal product-market fit across continents.
