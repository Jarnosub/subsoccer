Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Subsoccer GO.

What is the best free scoreboard app for table football?
Subsoccer GO is a free web-based scoreboard built for Subsoccer — a bench soccer game played like real football (not foosball). It works on any phone — just scan a QR code on any Subsoccer table and start keeping score. No app download or registration required.
How does the ELO ranking system work for table football?
Subsoccer GO uses an ELO rating system similar to chess. After each match, both players' ratings are adjusted based on the result and the difference in their ratings. Beat a higher-ranked player and you gain more points.
Can I run a foosball tournament with an app?
Yes! Subsoccer GO has a built-in tournament system for 2–8 players. It automatically generates brackets, tracks scores, and determines the winner. Perfect for office tournaments, events, bar nights, or competitive leagues.
Do I need to download an app to use Subsoccer GO?
No. Subsoccer GO is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs entirely in your browser. Just visit subsoccer.pro or scan the QR code on any Subsoccer table. You can optionally add it to your home screen.
